# StageGrid Systems

Industrial digital infrastructure for live production.

[stagegridsystems.com](https://stagegridsystems.com)

StageGrid Systems builds software and hardware for live production: rack planning, lighting control, and timecoded show playback. Closer to enterprise infrastructure and broadcast systems than consumer apps.

Production software for load-in through show call. Current products ship as betas with clear status.

## Products

| Product | Focus | Status | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **BuildYourRack** | Rack infrastructure | Open beta | [buildyourrackapp.com](https://buildyourrackapp.com) |
| **LogicDMX** | Lighting control and embedded | Closed alpha | [logicdmx.stagegridsystems.com](https://logicdmx.stagegridsystems.com) |
| **Phase** | Show playback and timecode | Early planning | [phase.stagegridsystems.com](https://phase.stagegridsystems.com) |

### BuildYourRack

Plan technical racks before the truck is loaded: U elevation, power, weight, and signal paths as documentation the crew can share.

### LogicDMX

DMX and lighting-controller hardware and software for fixtures, bridges, and patch routing. Currently in closed alpha with invited operators.

### Phase

Cross-platform playback and cue engine for timecoded productions. Frame-accurate timelines with LTC and MTC. Still in the first stage of early planning.

## How we work

- Reliability under show conditions, with predictable behavior when the audience is waiting.
- Synchronization across playback, lighting, and control, with one clock and one truth.
- Modularity so systems compose without fragile one-off integrations.
- Technical precision in numbers, timecode, patch data, and rack documentation.
- Infrastructure-grade design that reads like broadcast and industrial control, not consumer apps.
- Professional operational workflows where state is visible and errors are actionable.
